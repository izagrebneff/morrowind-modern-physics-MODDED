# Active ragdoll (experimental)
Replaces delayed handoff in b2934236d73e318c7fe1b46f09c43e244db4275a. Use the existing HRA hybridDeath Lua patch. At death create the ordinary articulated body immediately from the current pose, with gravity and collisions. Sample the chosen clip with private cloned keyframe controllers and a private clock, without attaching them to the visible skeleton. Only rotational targets are sampled: the torso and joints receive bounded angular impulses, while Bullet owns world translation, contact response, and constraints. No animation translations or forced final pose. Root torque is weaker than joint torque. Original fatal kick is omitted for marked clips to avoid doubling the animated reaction; unmarked deaths retain original behavior. Dremora exception retained. Drives expire on missing targets, completion, or timeout; drag/freeze suspend targets. Saved poses remain physical, not the animation clock. Exact clip trajectory cannot be retained when obstructed, and effects relying on clip text keys after death start are not replayed. Test melee/magic, cliff, wall, stairs, dragging, reload, other creatures. No in-game test yet.
